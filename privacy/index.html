Privacy Policy

Everyday Holiday for Android · Last updated 20 September 2026

About us

Everyday Holiday is a personal, non-commercial project. We are committed to keeping this project free and your data safe. You can reach us about anything in this policy at everydayholiday_inquiries@outlook.com.

The short version
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your choices in the app are stored on your phone, not by us.
  • Everyday Holiday runs no servers, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent to us.
  • Google Calendar sync is optional. If you use it, the app writes holidays into a calendar of its own in your Google account, and cannot see any of your other calendars.

What the app keeps on your phone

To save your holiday preferences, the app stores the following on your device:

  • which holiday calendars you have selected, and the order you have put them in
  • holidays you have marked as favourites or hidden
  • presets you have saved
  • a few settings, such as how calendar sync should group events
  • whether you have already seen the welcome note
  • the date the app last showed you today's holiday, so it only does so once a day
  • if you use Google Calendar sync, the progress of a sync while it runs and the result of the last one
  • if you use Google Calendar sync, the ID of the calendar the app created, so it can find it again
  • a fingerprint of the app's built-in holiday list, used only to notice when an update brings new content

None of this leaves your phone except through the routes below, all of which you control. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Google Calendar sync (optional)

If you choose to sync, the app asks your permission through Google's sign-in screen and then creates a calendar called "Everyday Holiday" in your Google account, filling it with the holidays you have selected — their names, short descriptions and a link to read more, taken from the app's built-in list. The app sets no reminders on those events: whether that calendar notifies you, and when, is a setting you control in Google Calendar itself.

The permission the app requests is limited to calendars it creates itself. It cannot see, change or delete your other calendars or the events in them. It does not request access to your name, email address or profile.

This data travels directly between your phone and Google. It never passes through anything run by Everyday Holiday. Once it is in your Google account, it is held under Google's Privacy Policy.

You can stop at any time: delete the "Everyday Holiday" calendar in Google Calendar, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing a holiday

When you tap Share, the app draws a picture of the holiday on your phone and hands it, with a short piece of text, to the app you pick — Instagram, WhatsApp, your messages or anything else in Android's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose where it goes, and it never passes through anything run by Everyday Holiday. What happens to it next is covered by the privacy policy of the app you shared it with. The picture is kept only in the app's temporary storage, and is replaced the next time you share.

Exporting your calendar

Export calendar file writes your selected holidays to a calendar file (.ics) and hands it to the app you pick, so you can open it in any calendar app, keep a copy, or send it to someone. The file holds the holidays themselves — names, dates, short descriptions and links — and nothing about you. It is kept only in the app's temporary storage, is replaced the next time you export, and goes nowhere until you choose where to send it.

Backups

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data — your selections, favourites, presets and settings — to your Google account, and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is a feature of Android handled by Google. We have no access to these backups. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or calendar permission. The app uses internet access for Google Calendar sync, and asks to show notifications only so a sync you started can show its progress while you use your phone for something else.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Links to other websites

Holidays link to Wikipedia and similar sources for further reading. These open in your browser, and the website you visit is covered by its own privacy policy.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to act on what you report, and we do not add you to any list.

Children

Everyday Holiday is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
