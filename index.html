Everyday Holiday

Every day is a celebration.

Thank you for checking out Everyday Holiday! There is so much joy to be shared from all around the globe, and our mission is to make those connections more accessible. We have catalogued national holidays, religious feasts, observances, name days and plenty of goofy novelties like World Gorilla Day and International Beer and Pizza Day.

Use this app to create a custom calendar prioritising the traditions that you find meaningful and important. With Everyday Holiday you’ll have something to celebrate every time the sun comes up!
